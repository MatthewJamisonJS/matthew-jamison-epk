// bio

matt jamison is from st. louis. he came up in church — dad was a minister — learned drums the right way, then bass, then beats. thirty-plus releases on bandcamp. plays live on the sp-404 a, no laptop. rails developer by day. session bassist for hire. ask him what sets him apart and he'll say: "when i make music, it's genuine." that's the whole thing.

he grew up in st. louis, in the church. his dad was a minister. music wasn't something he decided to do — it was just there. his cousin played the drums and matt watched. they wouldn't let him on yet because the rhythm wasn't there on the drumset. so he went and got it. concert band. drumline. back to the church kit. then bass guitar, playing with bands around the city, building connections with musicians and producers beyond st. louis.

this matters: he came to production through musicianship, not the other way around. he already knew how music felt in his body before he ever touched the sp-404 a.

in 2020 he joined sidechain society and started his llc. thirty-plus releases since — beat tapes, eps, collabs. the catalog is a record of someone doing the work because the work is the point.

he's also a session bassist. an audio engineer. a polyglot. a full-stack developer at concordia publishing house — rails 8, ~500k users, 77+ merged prs. his github bio: "learning how to create in a way that gives power to others." that's not copy. that's how he actually thinks about it.

his faith doesn't show up in the music as a message. it shows up as something steady underneath — the same way it's steady underneath everything else. he's a husband and a dad. he's still being formed. he'd tell you that last part himself, actually. it's one of the few things he'll say out loud without prompting.

he plays live on the sp-404 a. the original. a table, the machine, hands on pads. no laptop. the constraint is the point.

ask him what sets him apart: "when i make music, it's genuine." and then he stops talking. because that's the whole answer.

// why the beats sound like this

drums first. then bass. years of actually playing before the sp-404 a. the samples are from gospel records mostly. whatever was on when something happened that needed to be held onto. the sp-404 a has no undo. no screen. you make the call and live with it. that's the whole workflow. "inspiration is not just something you admire. it's something you answer. and answering it requires something simple, but hard: you gotta be quiet long enough to let it in." // still being formed.

// gear + rider  ·  sp-404 mkii

input list

  • 2× ¼" ts outputs from sp-404 a stereo main outs (l + r — no mono summing)
  • 1× monitor wedge or iem — stereo mix of sp-404 a output
  • 1× standard power outlet at table (sp-404 a can also run on batteries as backup)

stage needs

  • 1× 6-foot table at standing height (~42") — riser, hard case, or crate if venue tables are waist-high
  • set length: 15–30 min, adaptable
  • continuous — no breaks between tracks

what matthew brings

  • roland sp-404 a (self-provided — the original, not the mkii)
  • no laptop
  • no mixer or interface needed from venue
  • the machine is the stage plot

what matthew doesn't need

  • backline
  • an intro
  • more than one outlet
