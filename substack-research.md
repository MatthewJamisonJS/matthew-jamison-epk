## Voice and diction

- **Rhythm**: very short lines, one thought per line, heavy white space and `* * *` dividers. Fragments over sentences. Long book quotes carry whole posts; his own prose arrives in bursts between them. He curates as much as he writes.
- **Capitalization**: lowercase-leaning openings and headers ("what happened," "self-work," "feels ;-;"), but he capitalizes names, God-names, and scripture normally. Titles use code conventions: `_trauma`, `_love()`, `_whenWordsFail`.
- **Code diction bleeding into prose**: `` `choose the next action` `` in backticks, "context =", "new_question:", "digression →", "quick_bio:". Ampersands almost always over "and."
- **Emoji as punctuation**, especially 🖖🏿 (his signature sign-off), 😅 after hard admissions, 🥹🥲, 🫠, 🐇🕳️ for rabbit holes, 🙏🏿.
- **Direct address and blessing of the reader**: he prays for readers, asks them questions, thanks them for making it to the end.
- **Tone**: devotional, self-deprecating, unguarded, anti-performative. He confesses half-hearted prayers and apologizes mid-post for defaulting to "I." Never triumphalist — even the tornado post's triumph is supplied by a commenter, not him.
- **What he never does**: hype, announcements, selling, certainty, tidy endings, all-caps enthusiasm.
